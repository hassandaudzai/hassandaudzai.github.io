Privacy Policy

Manage Task

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Manage Task is a team task-management app provided by Hassan Daudzai. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Handle

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, and account creation information. Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in credentials.
  • Team information: team names, memberships, supervisor roles, and invitation information.
  • Task information: task titles, descriptions, assignments, priorities, statuses, due dates, and timestamps.
  • Comments: comments you post on tasks, associated author identifiers, and timestamps.
  • Support and deletion requests: information you provide when contacting support, and account identifiers, timestamps, and status information associated with an account deletion request.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, restore and sync teams across devices, display task assignments and comments, schedule reminders, respond to support requests, and process account deletion requests.

Information Shared Within Your Team

Members of a team can see information associated with that team, including member names, tasks, assignments, and comments. Supervisors can manage the team's tasks and delete comments. Share invitation codes privately because people with a valid code can use it to join the team.

Firebase Services

Manage Task uses Google Firebase Authentication for account access and Cloud Firestore for online account, team, task, comment, invitation, and deletion-request records. These services process information needed to provide their functionality and maintain service security.

See Privacy and Security in Firebase for information about Google's handling of Firebase data.

Firebase Diagnostic Data

Firebase automatically collects technical information such as device and operating-system details, app bundle identifiers, and development-platform information. Google uses this information to understand platform and version adoption and improve Firebase services. This diagnostic information is not linked to your account or a device identifier and is not used for advertising tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores local copies of profiles, teams, tasks, and comments, along with preferences such as appearance and reminder settings. Signing out does not necessarily remove all locally saved records.

Removing the app does not delete your online account or information stored in Firebase.

Photos, PDFs, and External Sharing

You can choose a camera photo, an existing photo, or a PDF when sharing a task. Files selected through this sharing feature are not uploaded as shared attachments to Manage Task's Firebase database. The app may create temporary local copies to prepare files for sharing.

When you share information through another app, that app and the recipients handle the shared copy. Their privacy policies apply. Deleting information from Manage Task does not delete copies already shared outside the app.

Permissions and Reminders

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Notification permission is requested for reminders. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Due-date reminders are scheduled locally on your device; open Manage Task to sync task changes and update reminders.

Account Deletion and Retention

You can request account deletion in the app using Delete Account. Requests are processed manually within seven days of the original request. Confirmation is sent to your account email after completion.

Deletion removes your authentication account and associated account data. Teams you supervise, including their online tasks and comments, are deleted. For teams you joined as a member, your membership and comments are removed and tasks assigned to you are unassigned; those teams remain available to their other members.

Devices that are offline may retain previously downloaded copies until they reconnect and the app processes the deletion. External copies shared through other apps are outside our control.

Information is otherwise retained as needed to provide the service. Service-provider backups or security records may follow the provider's retention practices.

Your Choices

You can update your profile name, manage reminder settings, control device permissions, and request account deletion. For questions about accessing or correcting your information, contact us using the address below.

Security

We use Firebase authentication and database access controls to restrict access to online information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials and invitation codes private.

Children's Privacy

Manage Task is intended for general team task management and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last-updated date.
