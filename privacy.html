Privacy Policy

Talim – Afghan Textbooks  |  Last updated: September 2026

Talim is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No accounts or registration required
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information collected
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only

The following is stored only on your device and never transmitted to any server:

  • Your language preference (Pashto or Dari)
  • Your last-read page for each book
  • Your bookmarks and favorite books
  • Downloaded PDF textbook files
  • Reading theme preference (light / sepia / dark)

All local data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for students of all ages, including children. We do not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
