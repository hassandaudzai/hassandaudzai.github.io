When Contacting Support

Describe the issue and include your device model, iOS version, and Manage Task version. Screenshots can help, but remove private information before sending them. Never send your password or team invitation code.

Restoring Your Teams

Sign in with the same Manage Task account while connected to the internet. Open each team to sync its tasks and comments. If a recently joined team is missing on another device, try signing out and signing in again.

Due-Date Reminders

Enable Due-Date Reminders in More → Settings and allow notifications in your device settings. Reminders apply to tasks assigned to you. Open the app and select the relevant team before the reminder time so task changes can sync.

Already scheduled reminders can appear while the app is closed. New assignments, comments, and status changes require opening the app to sync; they do not generate remote push notifications.

Request Account Deletion

In Manage Task, open More → Delete Account and follow the instructions. If you are on the Your Team setup screen, use its Delete Account option.

Requests are processed manually within seven days of the original request. Confirmation is sent to your account email after completion.

Teams you supervise, including their online tasks and comments, are deleted. Teams you joined remain available to their other members; your membership and comments are removed, and tasks assigned to you are unassigned.

If you cannot access your account, email support from your account email address. We may need to verify account ownership. Never email your password.
